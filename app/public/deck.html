The gap

Today the protocol's treasury is the only backstop.

Every Mezo user carries smart-contract risk with nowhere to hand it off. Of 155 Buildathon products, no other sells cover against exploits, halts or bridge failure, or lets veMEZO holders decide claims.

BACKSTOP turns that liability into a market: people who want protection pay for it, people who want yield carry it, and people with long-term skin in Mezo decide what is a valid claim.

How it works

Three roles. One pot of MUSD.

01

Buyers

Insure a veBTC lock, a trove's collateral, Mezo Swap pools, the MEZO bridge or a transaction halt. Premium in MUSD, 2–6% a year.

02

Underwriters

Deposit MUSD. Earn 85% of premiums plus Mezo's sMUSD savings yield on 90% of idle capital.

03

veMEZO assessors

Vote on claims, weighted by lock size and length. Share 5% of premiums and the bonds of rejected claims.

Built from Mezo's own parts

Every MUSD in the system does real work.

  • MUSDPremiums, underwriting capital, claim bonds, payouts, assessor rewards: demand on both sides.
  • sMUSDIdle underwriting capital earns the MUSD savings rate; yield is claimed into the pool.
  • TroveManager + PriceFeedTrove cover requires an active trove, capped by its collateral at MUSD's own price.
  • veBTCCover checks lock ownership and locked BTC; no over-insurance, even after a sale.
  • veMEZOA new job for MEZO: deciding claims, paid from premiums.

Enforced by the contracts

Insurance that can't be farmed.

  • Fully backedNo policy larger than free capital; underwriters can't withdraw what backs live cover.
  • No snipingPremiums vest over 7 days; withdrawals wait out a cooldown.
  • Votes can't be bought lateWeight is each veMEZO lock's power before filing. 60% supermajority, quorum, minimum voters.
  • No admin over moneyThe owner curates products and pauses sales. It cannot move funds or approve claims.
  • Claims never lostA paused sMUSD defers a payout, never cancels it; anyone can retry.
  • No circular coverMUSD's own core is excluded: MUSD can't insure MUSD.

Business model

A fee on every premium.

  • Protocol10% of premiums
  • Assessors5% of premiums + forfeited bonds
  • Underwriters85% of premiums + sMUSD yield
  • Market912.6 BTC locked as veBTC and $13.1M of MUSD loans (Mezo H1 2026). 5% of veBTC value covered at 3% ≈ $96k premiums a year, $9.6k to the protocol.
  • First buyersveBTC holders and borrowers exposed on Oct 5; treasuries that need cover to hold BTC on-chain.
