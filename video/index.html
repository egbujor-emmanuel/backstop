On October 5, an attacker drained 37 locked

bitcoin positions on Mezo, worth about $470,000. Mezo

paid everyone back from its own funds.

BACKSTOP is insurance for your money on Mezo.

You pay a small fee. If something goes

wrong, you get paid back in MUSD, Mezo's dollar.

Step 1. Open the app and connect your

wallet. It switches you to Mezo testnet for you.

Step 2. Pick what you want to protect,

type how much, and for how many days.

The app shows your price. Approve, buy, and

your policy appears, tied to your wallet.

Want to earn instead? Step 3. Deposit MUSD

into the pool. You earn the fees buyers

pay, plus Mezo's savings interest while your money waits.

If something goes wrong, step 4. File a

claim. Say how much you lost, link your

proof, and put down a small deposit, which

you get back unless the claim is rejected.

Step 5. People who hold veMEZO, Mezo's long-term

voters, check the proof and vote. If at

least 60% say yes, the pool pays you,

and the voters who got it right share a reward.

Everything you just saw is real, on Mezo

testnet. The Proof page lists every contract and

every transaction, so anyone can check it.
