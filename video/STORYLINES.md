# BACKSTOP pitch video — storylines

Length 1:50–2:10, 16:9 1920×1080 at 30 fps, narrated (the AKINDO form asks for a 2-minute pitch video), free
local Kokoro voice, real product footage captured from the live app. Every number traces to README.md,
proof/testnet-v2.jsonl or app/src/data.

## A. The bill that came due (recommended — a stranger follows one real claim from loss to payout)

- **Device / form:** a number lands, then one real claim travels through the product; object-to-object chain
  (each shot ends on the element that opens the next: the deposit → the policy → the claim card → the vote bar →
  the transaction hash).
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the October 5 loss printed as one number over Mezo's own data, the treasury
  as the only backstop, then BACKSTOP's real screens carrying DRILL-001 (150 MUSD) from purchase to an approved
  payout, ending on links anyone can click. It feels like evidence, not a promo.
- **First two seconds:** the real Replay page's positions table scrolling; `$472,476` stamps over it in amber,
  hazard stripe wiping in under it.
- **Beats:** loss number → treasury paid → BACKSTOP named → underwrite → buy cover → claim filed → veMEZO vote →
  payout → halt evidence → proof on-chain → claim line + end card.
- **Look:** the product's own ink #0b0d0c ground with its hazard-stripe motif as the one material; real UI at
  80–100% of the frame; Instrument Serif titles, Inter captions, amber only on the number, the payout and the mark.
- **Sound:** narration as the clock; no music source is signed in, so voice plus a near-silent tail; no synthetic
  tones.

## B. Twelve minutes

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day; split screen collapsing to one. 18:54 UTC on October 5: a veBTC
  holder's position drains on the left while, on the right, the same holder's BACKSTOP policy goes from claim to
  payout. **First two seconds:** a UTC clock reading 18:54:00 beside a veBTC balance. **Beats:** clock → drain →
  split → claim → vote → payout → collapse to one → end card. **Look:** two panes on ink, clock in JetBrains Mono.
  **Sound:** a ticking clock under the voice. Dropped: the holder's side would be a drawn reconstruction.

## C. Hazard stripe (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** type carries the argument; cut on the beat. Giant kinetic Instrument Serif lines on amber
  hazard-stripe fields, real UI only as 1-second inserts. **First two seconds:** "37 positions. 12 minutes." in
  300 px type, stripe slamming across. **Beats:** number → number → question → answer → three verbs → proof → mark.
  **Look:** amber/ink fields alternating per line. **Sound:** needs a music bed to carry the cuts, and none is
  available, so it would play flat.

**Picked: A** (the user's standing instruction is to decide and proceed).
